Courses Taught

Courses taught at IIT Bhubaneswar.

GRADUATE / UNDERGRADUATE

Surface Hydrology

Covers the fundamentals of the hydrologic cycle, precipitation, evapotranspiration, infiltration, and rainfall-runoff processes, along with streamflow analysis and conceptual/physically based catchment modeling.

GRADUATE / UNDERGRADUATE

Groundwater Hydrology

Covers groundwater occurrence and flow, aquifer properties, well hydraulics, groundwater recharge, and an introduction to numerical and machine-learning approaches for groundwater modeling.

LABORATORY

Hydraulics / Water Resources Engineering Laboratory

Hands-on laboratory course covering experiments in open-channel flow, pipe flow, hydraulic structures, and water resources measurement techniques.

UNDERGRADUATE

Engineering Drawing and Graphics

Foundational course in engineering drawing conventions, orthographic and isometric projections, and technical graphics for civil engineering students.
