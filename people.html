The researchers and students behind CHSRL — working across groundwater modeling, contaminant transport, and machine learning for hydrology.

PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Dr. Aatish Anshuman

Indian Institute of Technology Bhubaneswar

Aatish leads CHSRL's research on flow and transport processes in saturated and unsaturated zones and surface–subsurface water interactions — including groundwater recharge, contamination, and the impacts of land-use change on groundwater. He uses advanced numerical (meshless/meshfree) and machine-learning methods to investigate groundwater availability, and explores the influence of climate change on groundwater systems through interdisciplinary collaboration with hydrologists, remote-sensing experts, and geophysicists.
