About the Lab

Groundwater and surface water, studied as one system

The Connected Hydro Systems Research Lab (CHSRL), led by Dr. Aatish Anshuman at IIT Bhubaneswar, works on understanding flow and transport processes in both saturated and unsaturated zones, as well as surface–subsurface water interactions. Our research spans groundwater recharge, contamination source identification, and the impacts of land-use and climate change on groundwater availability. We employ advanced numerical methods (including meshless/meshfree techniques) and machine-learning models — such as LSTMs and swarm-based optimization — to investigate groundwater dynamics and quantify uncertainty. Through interdisciplinary collaboration with hydrologists, remote-sensing experts, and geophysicists, CHSRL works toward sustainable water management strategies for a changing climate.
