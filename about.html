About the Group

CHSRL's research approach, technical toolkit, and recognitions — from meshless numerical methods to machine learning for groundwater systems.

Research Focus

What the group works on, in practice.

Groundwater Flow & Contaminant Transport

Simulation of flow and reactive transport (including rate-limited adsorption) in highly heterogeneous saturated and unsaturated aquifers, using meshless/meshfree numerical methods.

Inverse Modeling & Contaminant Source ID

Encoder-decoder LSTM and surrogate-optimization workflows (multiverse optimization, particle swarm optimization) for identifying groundwater contamination release histories under uncertainty.

Surface Water–Groundwater Interaction

Conceptual rainfall-runoff modeling, streamflow simulation, and coupled surface-groundwater modeling benchmarked against models such as SWAT.

Climate Change & Recharge Dynamics

Studying groundwater recharge dynamics, snow-season soil water input, and the influence of a warming climate on groundwater systems in cold and monsoon-driven regions alike.
