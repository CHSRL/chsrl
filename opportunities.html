Careers

Opportunities

Motivated candidates are encouraged to reach out for research opportunities in groundwater modeling, contaminant transport, and machine learning for hydrology.

Why Join CHSRL

Real research problems

Work on groundwater and surface-water problems using advanced numerical and machine-learning methods, with results presented at HYDRO, ICCMS, AGU, and EGU.

Interdisciplinary collaboration

The group collaborates with hydrologists, remote-sensing experts, and geophysicists on groundwater and climate-related research.

Direct mentorship

Work closely with Dr. Aatish Anshuman and current PhD/Masters students across numerical modeling and machine learning for hydrology.

Research Internships

Summer & winter research internships

CHSRL regularly hosts research interns working alongside PhD and Masters students on groundwater and hydrology projects. If you're interested in hands-on experience with numerical or machine-learning approaches to hydrology, reach out with your CV and areas of interest.
